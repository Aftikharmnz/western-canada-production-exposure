# Enbridge liquids line guide — final review October 12, 2026

The map now separates a pipeline's published identity and commercial facts from the geometry available for it. The searchable guide covers the numbered Canadian Mainline/Lakehead sections and the pipeline assets/components in Enbridge's published Crude Oil and Liquids Pipelines portfolio, plus clearly identified former assets and public service references. Natural-gas transmission/distribution networks and storage volumes are outside this liquids-line inventory. An entry can represent a section, a combined service or a named system; the entry count is not a count of unique physical pipes.

Open **Market Pipes → Enbridge lines & systems**, or search for **Line 5**, **Line 7**, a named system, product or destination. Each guide card gives its published number/name, aliases, products, origin/destination, direction, capacity and basis, status evidence, dimensions, connections, trading context, source dates and official commercial links. Route cards also expose associated line numbers and links to those individual cards. Searching and reading a guide card preserves map selections.

The canonical facts are in [data/enbridge_liquids_lines.json](../data/enbridge_liquids_lines.json), independently published to the browser's data directory by `scripts/enbridge_lines.py`. `route_ids` join facts to a system context; they do not assign a numbered line to coordinates. Cards for lines without independently identified geometry open as information cards at the map centre without adding a guessed line, pin or highlight. Line 7 and Line 11 have separate regulator features selected by the exact CER `PipelineID` values `EnbridgeLine7` and `EnbridgeLine11`. Their former combined selection remains hidden as a legacy context reference. The published Line 11 geometry omits the Westover-to-Hamilton connection, which is stated on its card.

The principal sources are the [Q1 2026 operator configuration](https://www.enbridge.com/~/media/Enb/Documents/Reports/2026-LP-Customer-Handbook/2026_LPCH_ENB_pipeline_system_configuration.pdf?rev=026e5d92c852459f91d291606b17fcd3&hash=C832B01EA709A5D17646E50125964372), the [operator infrastructure catalogue](https://www.enbridge.com/~/media/Enb/Documents/Factsheets/FS_EnergyInfrastructureAssets.pdf?hash=0F0D0B4D79CA67E8A6F35606D00412B7&rev=25205c955e1840d3854fe621bb0200a6), whose cover is updated July 28, 2026, the [2025 annual report](https://www.enbridge.com/~/media/Enb/Documents/Investor-Relations/NoticeAndAccess/2026/ENB_2025_Annual_Report_EN.pdf?hash=3AC33A8767949550990ACA7684ED7772&rev=b158e24fe41d45f5af4f3178958a071e), and specific current operator/regulator sources linked in every record. The global catalogue update date does not establish the date of every individual operating claim. The review date is kept separate from all source dates.

Important interpretations retained in the data:

- Line 5 carries light crude, light synthetic crude and NGLs. It is not identified as a gasoline/diesel or heavy-crude line. Its reviewed September 28 operator update describes operation through a temporary bypass; the 540,000 bbl/d published annual-average rating is not a verified available-capacity rating for that bypass.
- Line 3A and Line 93, Line 2A and Line 2B, and Line 78A and Line 78B are distinct published sections. Former Line 6B is searchable through the Line 78 aliases. Connected section ratings are not added into an end-to-end capacity.
- Lines 14/64 remain one expressly combined published service/rating. The 343,000 bbl/d rating is not duplicated for each number.
- Southern Lights is Line 13, carrying diluent northbound. Its 198,000 bbl/d post-expansion rating from the 2025 annual report takes precedence over the older 180,000 bbl/d statement retained in the asset catalogue.
- Norman Wells is Line 21. Its historical rating does not imply current production or throughput after the source-production shut-in in August 2026.
- Contradictory Line 62 direction descriptions and Line 8 product/service descriptions remain qualified rather than silently resolved. Unknown capacities stay unknown; section-dependent capacities retain their separate scope in text.
- The former Enbridge Olympic label is corrected using BP's operator plan. Tinsley's historical Enbridge ownership and crude service are distinguished from an unverified current pipeline operator. An archived petroleum-products GIS classification alone does not establish current refined-product service.
- Line 95 is identified as Sarnia to Shell Take-Off, with a 20-inch diameter, in the operator's February 2025 map. Its current products, capacity and operating status remain unverified; a map label does not establish commercial service.

Trading links lead to official tariffs, service levels, commodity specifications and relevant operator updates. Ratings are neither actual flows nor bookable space. The app does not infer current batches, nominations, apportionment, customer-specific tariffs, maintenance restrictions or terminal connections from location. Some official shipper resources require customer access.

Validation rejects duplicate identities, unsupported fields, unsafe source URLs, missing dates, nonpositive/nonfinite capacities, invalid source-to-publication changes, unknown route references and embedded geometry in the catalogue. The targeted Line 7/11 update validates publisher IDs before accepting geometry and preserves every unrelated feature's coordinates. Browser checks cover individual number searches, persistent cards, keyboard access and scrolling within the mobile map.
